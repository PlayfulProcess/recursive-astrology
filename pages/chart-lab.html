A chart, through the schools

Read the sky to know yourself, not to be told your fate. A chart is a mirror and a calendar, not a command. Relate to the symbol; never obey it.

Every reading here is a possibility: this could mean one thing; it could also mean another; what does it mean to you? Each school speaks in its own voice (old books speak in certainties) and carries its label and its source. The readings come from this site's own files. Two things reach out, and only when you ask: Interpret with AI sends the recursive.eco assistant a message you see and edit first, and a chart opened from the calculator asks the chart server for its transits (positions only, never birth data). If you are signed in on recursive.eco in this browser, the chart menu also lists your saved charts from your account; one you pick is recomputed by the same chart server, with only its id in the address, never its birth data.
