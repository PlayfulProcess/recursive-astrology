A chart, through the schools

Read the sky to know yourself, not to be told your fate. A chart is a mirror and a calendar, not a command. Relate to the symbol; never obey it.

Every reading here is a possibility: this could mean one thing; it could also mean another; what does it mean to you? Each school speaks in its own voice (old books speak in certainties) and carries its label and its source. This page reads only this site's own files: no server, no assistant, no account.
