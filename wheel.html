Nothing here is drawn at random — the positions are your own chart. Click a house. Each opens its position prompt from the casting grammar and its meaning from the flagship interpretation set. Resolving your cusps and planets needs a real chart calculation (birth time + place); until that is wired into the app, the wheel reads honestly as the twelve standing rooms of a life — enter placements you already know, or simply reflect house by house. Data: grammars/casting-twelve-houses/grammar.json. The creed holds on every surface: a chart is a mirror and a calendar, never a command. The All Houses frame (on by default — glowing sectors) also stacks whichever grammars you pick above (every house-bearing voice, by default — Ptolemy's Tetrabiblos, the Victorian skeptic Proctor, Alan Leo, the Canonical Western set, and the generated Astro-of-all-astros synthesis) for the house you tap — read live from grammars/_collection.json, matched by house number the same way Archetypal Astrology matches planets. The picker highlights which grammars actually have house content and lets you narrow the stack to just the voices you want. See docs/DESIGN-wheel-frames.md.
