Bodygraph

Nine centres, thirty-six channels, sixty-four gates — drawn by hd-render.js, the same renderer the chart calculator uses. Light a gate, a channel or a centre, and take the drawing away as SVG or PNG.

The URL is the state: ?gates=29, ?gates=20-34&mode=channel, ?gates=sacral&mode=center, plus &design=, &reading=center, &legend=0, &theme=dark. The contract is written down in docs/HD-RENDER.md.
