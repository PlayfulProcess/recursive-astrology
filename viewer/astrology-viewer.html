About these Human Design descriptions

The structure is the system’s; the words are ours. Gate numbers on the King Wen hexagram sequence, the 36 channels, the nine centres and the rules that derive type, authority and profile are the Human Design system’s public correspondences — public record, and the same in any correct implementation. The positions are computed here from this project’s own Skyfield/DE421 ephemeris.

Where the system comes from. Human Design was synthesised by Ra Uru Hu (born Alan Krakower) from 1987 onward, drawing on the I Ching, Western astrology, the chakra model and the Kabbalistic Tree of Life. Its published descriptive literature is his authorship and is not reproduced here.

Every description below was written for this project. Gate names, keynotes, channel names, centre blurbs and the type notes are ours, not quoted or adapted from Human Design publications. Each gate is anchored instead to its I Ching hexagram — Chinese title and pinyin from the Zhouyi, whose English reference here is James Legge’s 1882 translation (public domain).

Read it as a mirror. These are configurations people report recognising, not statements about what will happen. Where the tradition gives a “strategy”, it is set out here as an experiment somebody might run, never as an instruction to obey.
